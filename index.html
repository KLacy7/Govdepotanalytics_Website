Company Background

Real delivery experience. Senior-level review. No handoffs.

GovDepot Analytics pairs hands-on data execution with senior consulting oversight to solve federal agencies' toughest Excel automation, logistics analysis, and business intelligence challenges.

Kevin White leads delivery. As a Business Development Representative and Account Executive at Truckstop.com — one of logistics tech's most data-intensive platforms — he made data-driven decisions from a combination of historical sales data and his daily, weekly, and monthly sales cadence: calls, emails, and follow-ups. That discipline increased sales and gave him a deep, working understanding of trucking company metrics, goals, and bottom line. He holds the Google Professional Certification in Data Analysis and builds solutions with advanced Excel, Power Query, SQL, and visualization tools.

Portfolio on GitHub: Kevin's applied analytics work includes a trucking-company operations analysis (Logistics_1000) and a Philadelphia SEPTA bus network analysis — projects that surfaced operational inefficiencies, route imbalances, and major delay and detention issues.

Doe Ocansey serves as senior consultant, data scientist, and quality assurance lead, reviewing every deliverable for analytical accuracy, methodological soundness, and federal compliance before it reaches your desk.
